COMMANDS

Simple controls.

NEXORA - Música is designed around simple commands that are easy to understand and fast to use.

/play Play a song
/buscar Search for music
/skip Skip current song
/pause Pause playback
/resume Resume playback
/queue View queue
/shuffle Shuffle queue
/volume Change volume
/loop Configure loop
/playlist Manage playlists
