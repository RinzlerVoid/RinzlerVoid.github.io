Your server. Your rules.

KRYVEX gives your staff the control they need without making moderation unnecessarily complicated.

/warn Issue moderation warnings
/timeout Temporarily restrict members
/kick Remove members from the server
/ban Permanently ban members
/history Review moderation history
/kryvex setup Configure the audit log channel
/kryvex anti_nuke Protect channels and roles from destructive bursts
/kryvex anti_raid Detect join spikes and restrict new accounts
/kryvex language Choose Spanish or English server responses
/kryvex invite_filter Enable Discord invite filtering
/kryvex warn_policy Configure warning escalation
/backup_create Create a safe structure backup
/backup_list List and restore safe structure backups
